# sendspin-guition

Sendspin player for Guition devices with Line Out.

Not many devices include Line Out, but Guition has made two: the 1.80" silver puck and a new version of the rotary dial "knob" that I am currently waiting to receive.

These devices are perfect for Sendspin players because of their Line Out support through the 3.5 mm mini-jack connector.
